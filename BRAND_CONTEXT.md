## 3. Mission, vision, values

**Mission.** We build brands that move with meaning. Nami exists to give founders the load-bearing brand infrastructure most agencies skip (identity, messaging, content systems, automation), assembled into something a business can actually run on.

**Vision (3-year, to mid-2029).** A one-to-three-person studio at **£15k+ MRR** from 6–10 active retainer clients, plus a productised "Nami Automation" engagement that runs without Joe in the room. The go-to for *growing* founders past product-market fit who now need infrastructure to scale without burning. Not the cheapest, not the loudest, the one that compounds.

**North Star metric.** Monthly recurring revenue (MRR). Every other metric (followers, leads, impressions) is downstream of it.

**The promise.** *"You don't need another agency. You need a partner that builds with intent."* Functional translation: every engagement leaves the client more self-sufficient than it started. No agency-dependency by design.

### The three values

1. **Branding is infrastructure.** Identity systems aren't decoration. They're the load-bearing structure for content, marketing, and growth. Build the foundation properly and everything on top moves faster.
2. **Clarity over volume.** Sharp positioning beats more output every time. Better to ship one piece that lands than five that drift. Volume is what you earn after clarity, not before.
3. **Systems compound, posts decay.** Anyone can ship a post. Few build engines. Design for the second order: formats, frameworks, and pipelines so today's work makes tomorrow's easier.

### What Nami says no to

One-off social posts disconnected from a content system. "Refresh the logo" briefs with no strategy underneath. Pure execution where Nami can't influence the system. Cookie-cutter packages that ignore the brand's actual state. Pitching against agencies whose only edge is being cheaper.

### Three claimable diagnostic frameworks (owned terms)

Nami has named three diagnostic conditions across its insight articles. These are intentionally claimable terms (low-competition SEO + AI Overview citation candidates):

1. **Brand Decay.** The four-shape diagnostic for how healthy brands fall apart: tonal decay, visual decay, promise decay, system decay. Article: `/insights/brand-decay`.
2. **The Friction Tax.** The hidden cost paid by businesses past survival but still run like startups. Article: `/insights/the-friction-tax`.
3. **Audience Without Architecture.** Why a growing audience isn't growing revenue; the path through the audience is the asset, not the audience itself. Article: `/insights/audience-without-architecture`.

## 7. Process: four phases

1. **Discovery + positioning** (Weeks 1–2). Stakeholder and customer interviews, market and competitor audit, positioning workshop and statement, messaging architecture draft.
2. **Design + build** (Weeks 3–6). Visual identity and design tokens, content format and platform plan, conversion-led website build, brand book and applied guidelines.
3. **Launch + integrate** (Week 7). Live site and analytics, lead-capture and nurture flows, content and ops handoff, launch sequence and comms.
4. **Scale + evolve** (ongoing partnership). Monthly creative direction, content pipeline support, conversion and funnel iteration, new design work as needed.

Tight enough to ship in eight weeks, deep enough to keep working two years on.

## 9. Brand voice

The voice is the whole product. Match it in any user-facing copy.

**Energy.** Confident and direct. No hedging, make the call. Slightly combative against trends and decoration-without-function: Nami names what it disagrees with. Strategic and pragmatic, outcome-focused. Energetic and bold: "move fast, think loud, refuse to stay boxed in." Stops short of bombast; the confidence is earned by substance.

**Cadence.** Short, punchy sentences for emphasis. Periods-as-rhythm at anchor moments. Function-first: aesthetics serve trust, traction, and emotional pull, never the reverse.

**Imagery.** Motion and water: waves, momentum, current, chop, flow, carry, scale. Lean into the *Nami = wave* root without overplaying it.

**Tone by surface.** Website: confident and declarative, signature phrases lean in. LinkedIn: conversational and sharp, opinion-led, no "thought leader" cliches. Instagram: warmer, visual-first, accessible, curatorial (the NAMI Up North voice updated, spotlighting NE creative scene). Email/newsletter: personal and narrative, less promotional. Client comms and proposals: direct, scope-first, no advisory padding.

### Signature phrases (verbatim, quote don't paraphrase)

Live canon as of May 2026 reconciliation:

- "We Build Brands That Move With Meaning."
- "Your business shouldn't depend on you being in every room." *(current site hero)*
- "Self-sustaining creative ecosystems for founders." *(current site subhead, repeated across surfaces)*
- "Waves of Creative Impact."
- "Clarity Over Chaos."
- "Creative With Weight."
- "Systems That Carry You."
- "Still Stitching Your Brand Together As You Go?"
- "We help brands find their shape again."
- "Nothing we do is plug-and-play. Everything is made to move together."
- "You don't need another agency. You need a partner that builds with intent."

**Retired phrase:** *"Creative systems built for real-world momentum."* Was a signature phrase through 2023–2024. Replaced by the "Your business shouldn't depend on you being in every room" + "Self-sustaining creative ecosystems" framing during the May 2026 copy overhaul. Do not use in new copy.

### Hard rules

- **Zero em dashes. Anywhere.** Site copy, testimonials, FAQs, case studies, Creative Waves articles, email, social. Replace with periods, commas, colons, semicolons, or restructure. En dashes (–) and hyphens (-) are fine. Grep for the em-dash character before saving any copy.
- **Banned phrases:** never write "no fluff" or "no filler."
- **Banned vocabulary:** "synergy", "unlock", "unleash", "craft", "leverage", "award-winning" (until there's a real award), "industry-leading", "best in class", "boutique agency", "we're passionate about", "in today's fast-paced world", "in summary".
- **No exclamation marks. No emojis** unless Joe asks.
- **No apology framing** ("sorry to bother", "just checking in"). **No long preambles.** **No advisory hedging** ("you might want to consider"); just say what to do.
- **No "senior partner" as a role title.** "Creative partner" is the canon. ("Senior-only output", "senior-led" as quality descriptors are fine.)
- **No "same hands" as marketing copy.** Reads as jack-of-all-trades. Reserve for the About-page founder manifesto where context ("senior-only output, no middle layer") clarifies the intent. Elsewhere use "one creative partner", "built as one studio", "integrated at strategy, not stitched at handoff".

**Voice check before publishing:** Does it make a clear point or hedge it? Is there a shorter way to say it? Does it sound like a confident operator or a polite intern? Any em dashes? Would a competitor agency publish it verbatim (if yes, sharpen)?

**Gradient highlight rule.** Pink magenta gradient on text (`text-gradient` class) applies only to the final 1–4 punch words at the end of a heading. Never on whole clauses, never on prepositions or articles. The gradient must earn the highlight by being the line that pays off the heading.

### Joe's personal comms style (separate from brand voice)

Direct, action-first, terse. Trusts recommendations: when given a decision, surface the choice plus a pick and proceed, rather than waiting for permission.

> The canonical magenta `#ff00bc` is now reflected across both the codebase and the Notion brand guidelines (reconciled 22 May 2026). Soft and deep variants (`#ff66db`, `#800060`) are derived for the wave gradient flow. Shadows use `rgb(255 0 188 / X)` consistently across the codebase.

**Typography.** Primary: Instrument Sans (body, UI, headings). Headlines lean condensed and confident; body stays clean and readable.

**Imagery direction.** High contrast, minimal saturation outside brand magenta and cyan. Motion implied through composition (diagonals, leading lines, visible flow). Avoid stock photography; lean abstract textures, brand graphics, or real client work. Video is vertical-first and captioned, no music unless purposeful.

**Logo.** Full "Nami Creative" wordmark (`public/Nami-Logo.png`). Only on pure white, pure black, or near-black (`#0e0f11`). Minimum clear space equals the height of the "N".

**Site visual direction.** Liquid atmosphere, flowing magenta accent with cyan secondary, magnetic CTAs, custom cursor, scroll-reveal letter animations, wave video in the hero. Mobile-first with iOS-grade polish. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`: motion is informational, never decorative noise.

**Wave gradient animation.** The `text-gradient` class animates the magenta sweep across 24 seconds, symmetric color stops (deep → accent → soft → accent → deep) at 0/25/50/75/100% so the loop wraps invisibly. Slow, liquid, fluid-metal motion, not a swipe.

100 people in, and this is just the start.

Nice one for being here at the start of the NAMI Creative Network.

The support and interest around this has been incredible already. I have met some ridiculously talented people through NAMI, and it has reminded me why I wanted to build this properly in the first place.

I love nothing more than seeing good people win. If I can look back in six months and see collaborations, projects, commissions and friendships that happened because of what we are creating now, that will honestly be the best thing ever for me.

So if you are reading this, you are in the right place. There is loads to look forward to. Stay close, share your work, tag NAMI in what you are building, and tell the canny people around you to get involved too. This only works if we build it together.

Useful bit

Audience is only useful when it has somewhere to go

A growing audience can make it feel like the business is moving. Sometimes it is. Sometimes it is just attention with no clear path behind it.

The audience is potential energy. The path through it is what turns that attention into enquiries, bookings, sales or opportunities.

If people find you through Instagram, TikTok, a podcast, a referral or a local feature, the next step should feel obvious. One destination. One offer. One action. A quick way to capture interest before intent fades.

This week, walk the journey from your latest post to your enquiry page on your phone. If the path asks people to make five decisions before they can act, that is where the work is.

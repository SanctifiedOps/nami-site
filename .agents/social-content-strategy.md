# NAMI Creative — Social Content Strategy

*Strategy document. Lives in repo so future Claude sessions and any future Notion sync know the social playbook. Originally drafted 2026-05-22.*

## Context

NAMI Creative is restarting social presence across four platforms simultaneously. Joe built a warm community brand called *NAMI Up North* in 2021 spotlighting the North East creative scene, grew to ~2,300 IG followers, then went quiet through and after Covid. He has rebranded to NAMI Creative (studio) and is gently reactivating the same audience. 2,000 story views in a single day of sharing confirms the audience is still there.

The strategic problem: reactivate a community brand without burning the studio brand. The community channel (Instagram, with TikTok + Facebook downstream) stays value-first and curation-led. The studio sales channel (LinkedIn) carries the conversion weight. The two channels feed each other but speak differently.

This document covers: platform roles, content pillars, format-per-platform, weekly cadence under a light (3-5hr/week) time budget, a first-month content calendar, a hooks library, algorithm + psychology levers per platform, and the skills invoked at each step.

**Locked parameters:**
- **Time budget:** Light, 3-5 hours/week. One batched session weekly.
- **Reel format:** Mix of (a) on-camera vlog-style at NE locations, (b) designed kinetic-typography reels.
- **Brand spotlight depth:** Shared NAMI template, lightly customised per brand. ~30 min per spotlight. Weekly cadence.
- **Service surfacing on community channels (IG/TikTok/FB):** Subtle integration only. Bio links carry the funnel. Service mentions ~1 in 10 posts at most. LinkedIn carries the direct sales.

## Platform strategy — four channels, four distinct roles

| Channel | Primary role | Tone | Posting target | Funnel position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Community anchor. Curation + value. Warm-audience reactivator. | NAMI Up North voice updated: confident, curatorial, slightly insider, slightly cheeky. Speaks AS a member of the NE creative scene, not above it. | 4-5 feed posts/week + 2-3 reels/week + 7-10 stories/day | Top of funnel. Bio link to namicreative.co.uk. Soft funnel via story polls + DM conversation. |
| **TikTok** | Reel amplification + native short-form experiments. | Slightly looser than IG. More raw, less polished. Hooks-first. | 3-4 short videos/week (mostly IG reel cross-posts + 1-2 native) | Top of funnel. Discovery surface. Bio link. |
| **Facebook** | Passive cross-post. Maintain presence, don't invest creative effort. | Same as IG (it's a mirror). | Cross-post via Meta Business Suite (auto). | Bottom-tier. Local discovery and older-demographic catch. |
| **LinkedIn** | Sales pipeline. Direct studio-voice. Frameworks, case studies, insights, soft offer. | Studio-voice (confident, direct, framework-led). Closer to the namicreative.co.uk register than the IG community register. | 3-4 posts/week (mix of carousels + text + native video) | Mid-bottom funnel. Direct CTA to /offers/flow-funnel, /work, /insights. |

**Why these roles work specifically for NAMI:**

- Instagram already has 2,300 warm followers who knew NAMI Up North. The community channel is where they live and what they consented to. Selling at them risks burning the brand. Curating + adding value continues the contract.
- LinkedIn is a separate audience (founders, agency leads, in-house marketers, fractional CMOs, B2B buyers). They never knew NAMI Up North. They meet NAMI Creative as a studio. Direct sales register is appropriate there.
- TikTok and Facebook are amplification surfaces, not destinations. Production effort goes into IG and LinkedIn; the other two get repurposed versions.

## Format playbook per platform

### Instagram

**Feed posts (carousels):**
- 5-9 slide carousels. First slide = hook (4-6 word title + visual). Last slide = soft CTA (tag someone, save for later, link in bio).
- Slide 1 hook patterns: declarative claim, contrarian take, identity-callout ("if you're a [persona]…"), single-word punch.
- Slides 2-7 = body. One idea per slide. Periods-as-rhythm at anchor moments.
- Save-rate is the algorithm-favoured signal. Tactic: make slide N-1 a value-dense summary ("save this for later when you're working on X").

**Reels:**
- Two formats running in parallel:
  - **(a) On-camera vlog reels:** Joe at a NE location, ~30-60 sec, hook in first 2 sec, one specific insight or spotlight, no music over voice. Format: "I'm at [location]. Here's what [brand] is doing that nobody's talking about." Or: "I'm walking past [thing]. Here's what it taught me about [brand topic]."
  - **(b) Designed reels:** Kinetic typography, 15-30 sec, no voice (or VO), strong visual rhythm. Best for hot takes and framework reveals. Tools: CapCut, Canva, or hand-animated in After Effects.
- 3-Second Rule applied: visual hook + verbal hook (if VO) + on-screen text hook all hit in the first second.

**Stories:**
- Daily light cadence (10-15 min/day):
  - 3-5 reposts of NE creatives Joe is spotlighting (reinforces community + triggers their tag-back)
  - 2-3 polls/Q&As (engagement = algorithm signal + audience research)
  - 1-2 BTS frames (studio work, vlog clips, location moments)
  - 1 soft funnel pointer per week (story link sticker to /offers/flow-funnel or a Creative Waves article)

**Highlights:** Three permanent highlights:
- "Spotlights" — archive of every NE brand spotlight
- "Studio" — BTS of NAMI Creative's work
- "Creative Waves" — links to insights articles

### TikTok

- **Native cadence: 3-4 short videos/week.** Two are IG reel cross-posts. One or two are native (raw, vertical-only, lower production).
- TikTok-specific format: shorter (7-15 sec max for native), heavier on text overlay, often borrows trending sounds (algorithmic boost).
- Treat TikTok as a discovery surface. Bio link to namicreative.co.uk. Comments matter, reply to every one in the first hour.
- TikTok handles search differently from IG. Captions should include keyword phrases ("how to convert followers", "newcastle creative studio", "brand strategy") that match search intent.

### Facebook

- **Auto cross-post from IG via Meta Business Suite.** Zero additional creative effort.
- Don't post natively. Don't engage with FB comments unless something major (a customer enquiry).
- Maintain the page for trust + older-demographic discoverability + Google Knowledge Panel association.

### LinkedIn

- **Cadence: 3-4 posts/week.** Roughly: 1 carousel, 1 native video or reel-clip, 1-2 text posts.
- **PDF native carousels are the top-performing format on LinkedIn in 2026.** 8-12 slides. First slide = hook + audience callout. Last slide = explicit CTA (read the article, book the call, see the case study).
- **Text posts:** 1200-1500 chars. Hook in first 2 lines (before the "see more" cut). Use line breaks every 1-2 sentences. End on a question or a soft call ("comment if you've seen this happen").
- **Native video:** 30-90 sec, captions burned in (most watch without sound). Founder-voice. Camera or screen-record.
- **Skip:** YouTube embeds (LinkedIn penalises external video), excessive hashtags (3 max), generic engagement bait.
- **Engagement discipline:** Reply to every comment within the first 2 hours of posting. The first 60 minutes determine algorithmic distribution.

## Weekly workflow (the 3-5 hour budget broken down)

Single batching philosophy: do it in chunks, not in dribbles. Three sessions per week.

```text
MONDAY     90 min   LinkedIn batch:
                    - 1 carousel (designed in Figma or Canva)
                    - 2-3 text posts drafted
                    - Schedule via LinkedIn native scheduler or Buffer

WEDNESDAY  60 min   Instagram batch:
                    - 2-3 feed carousels (1 spotlight + 1-2 tips)
                    - Scripts for 1-2 reels
                    - Caption + first-comment hooks
                    - Schedule via Meta Business Suite (auto-posts to FB)

FRIDAY     60 min   Reel filming + edit:
                    - Film 1-2 on-camera reels (vlog or talking head)
                    - Edit + caption
                    - Cross-post to TikTok via CapCut export

DAILY      10-15min Stories + comment replies:
                    - Reshare 2-3 NE creator stories
                    - 1-2 BTS frames
                    - Reply to comments on yesterday's posts
                    - Check DMs

Total:     ~4hr/week
```

**Quarterly review (every 3 months, 2 hours):**
- Pull data: which posts hit, which didn't
- Update pillar ratio if one pillar dramatically outperforms
- Refresh hooks library based on what landed
- Identify new NE brands to spotlight next quarter

## Psychology levers in play

Five named levers, mapped to specific tactical use.

**1. Reciprocity** (give first, no ask)
- Every spotlight post = NAMI giving free promotion. Recipients want to give back (tag, share, mention). The community flywheel starts spinning.
- Tactic: at minimum 35% of feed is reciprocity content. Don't dilute by adding a sales tail.

**2. Unity** (shared identity)
- "NE creative" identity is the in-group NAMI speaks AS, not AT.
- Tactic: insider language ("we know what it's like up here"), shared landmarks, in-jokes about Tyneside, never patronising. NAMI is a member, not an outsider observer.

**3. Authority by curation**
- The person who decides who gets spotlighted becomes the authority who matters.
- Tactic: be selective. Spotlight quality work, not every account. Becoming "the NAMI bump" is a status NAMI earns by being discriminating.

**4. Social proof (loop)**
- Spotlighted brands tag back. Their followers see NAMI. Some follow. Next week's spotlight goes to one of them. Recursive growth loop.
- Tactic: always tag the spotlighted brand on slide 1 AND in the caption (separately, for algorithm).

**5. Loss aversion / scarcity** (used only on LinkedIn + occasional IG soft sells)
- Flow Funnel cap: "4 builds open this month" creates real scarcity (already true).
- Tactic: monthly LinkedIn post showing the cap + slots remaining + last month's results. Quarterly IG post for the same.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:**
- Don't break Reciprocity by tail-ending spotlights with a sales pitch. Spotlight ends with the brand's tag, full stop.
- Don't dilute Unity by speaking ABOUT the NE creative scene as an outsider would. Speak FROM inside it.
- Don't degrade Authority by spotlighting weak work for the volume.
- Don't manufacture Scarcity (only use it when the cap is real).

## Measurement + iteration

### Monthly metrics dashboard (5 numbers, takes 15 min to pull)

| Metric | Where | Target by end of month 3 |
|---|---|---|
| IG follower growth | IG Insights | +5% per month from baseline |
| IG story view rate | IG Insights | >40% of follower count (already there) |
| IG save rate | IG Insights | Average >2% of impressions on carousels |
| LinkedIn impressions | LinkedIn analytics | +15% per month |
| Bio-link clicks to namicreative.co.uk | GA4 referral report | Steady upward trend |

### Kill criteria

After 3 months of consistent posting:
- If IG follower growth is below 1% per month, the pillar ratio needs rebalancing (likely too much soft sell, not enough spotlight)
- If LinkedIn impressions are flat, the carousel format isn't working, try native video as the primary
- If bio-link clicks are zero, the bio CTA isn't pulling, rewrite the bio
- If no NE creators are tagging NAMI back within 6 weeks of spotlighting them, the reciprocity loop isn't firing, check whether spotlight tone is generous or self-promoting

### What to track in a private file

Spreadsheet or Notion table: every post, date, platform, pillar, hook used, saves, comments, link clicks. Quarterly review = sort by save rate (IG) and dwell time (LinkedIn) and write a 5-bullet "what's working" memo.

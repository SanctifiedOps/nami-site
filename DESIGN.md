# NAMI Creative design context

## Design read

A preservation-first evolution of a North East creative consultancy and community platform. The visual language should feel like an independent editorial studio: dark, confident, locally grounded and built around the work of real people. NAMI magenta is a precise signal, not ambient decoration.

## Design dials

### Public brand and Network pages

- Design variance: 7/10
- Motion intensity: 4/10
- Visual density: 5/10

### Member and admin product pages

- Design variance: 4/10
- Motion intensity: 2/10
- Visual density: 6/10

## Preserve

- Near-black foundation and white typography.
- NAMI magenta as the primary accent.
- North East photography and member imagery.
- Instrument Sans unless typography testing proves a second family adds a clear editorial role.
- Direct copy, local references and human tone.
- Strong scale contrast in major headlines.
- Existing routes, content, analytics, metadata and structured data.

## Change

- Reduce glow, blur, gradients and translucent surfaces.
- Stop using the same centred full-height hero on unrelated pages.
- Replace repeated equal card grids with layouts shaped by the content.
- Reserve pills for status, filters, taxonomy and compact actions.
- Reduce decorative motion and remove pointer-following effects.
- Use stronger section transitions through scale, alignment, rules, media and whitespace.
- Give marketing, editorial, directory and product surfaces distinct but related layout grammars.
- Create a dedicated app shell for member and admin pages.

## Core visual system

### Colour

- Surface 0: near black, used for the main canvas.
- Surface 1: a small elevation step for panels that need separation.
- Foreground: warm white.
- Muted foreground: accessible grey for supporting copy.
- Accent: NAMI magenta, used for primary actions, active state, focus and selected emphasis.
- Secondary colours: only for functional meaning such as success, warning, error and data series.

Avoid decorative cyan unless it has a defined role. Avoid large pink light blooms behind content.

### Typography

- Use a small, named set of roles: display, page title, section title, card title, lead, body, small UI and metadata.
- Keep long-form reading between roughly 65 and 75 characters per line.
- Avoid interface copy below 12px.
- Use uppercase and wide tracking sparingly for metadata, not every section introduction.
- Prefer weight, scale and spacing over coloured headline fragments.

### Spacing

- Base unit: 4px.
- Common steps: 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96 and 128px.
- Public pages may use larger editorial gaps.
- Product screens should use denser 16, 24 and 32px groupings.
- Every section should have a clear internal rhythm rather than inheriting a default large vertical padding.

### Shape

- Default radius: 8px for controls and small panels.
- Large media or feature panel radius: 12px.
- Full pills only for compact statuses, filters, avatars and primary actions where the brand calls for them.
- Square editorial frames and hairline divisions are valid and should be used deliberately.

### Motion

- Default transition: 160 to 240ms.
- Page entrance motion should be limited to the hero or a single key composition.
- No pointer-following light fields, card tilt or universal hover lift.
- Image zoom is reserved for galleries and editorial media where it communicates clickability.
- Reduced-motion mode must show the final state immediately.

## Surface families

### Consultancy

Editorial and outcome-led. Larger type, fewer containers, case studies with specific evidence, and controlled use of local imagery.

### Creative Network

People and work first. Member faces, portfolio images, events and stories should create the visual energy. The interface around them should stay quiet.

### News hub

Magazine hierarchy rather than a uniform blog grid. Lead story, current issue or theme, section navigation, varied story sizes and strong reading widths.

### Member area

Task-led and reassuring. Clear progress, saved state, plain controls, reliable feedback and a compact responsive shell.

### Admin area

Operational and dense. Prioritise queues, exceptions and decisions. Decoration should never compete with status or action.
